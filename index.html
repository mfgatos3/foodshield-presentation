Quantitative & Empirical Integration

Integrated Scientific & Socio-Spatial Evidence

∑ Bio-Thermal Mathematical Model

⚛ Technoscience
Newton's Law of Cooling (Thermal Dissipation):
$$T(t) = T_{\text{amb}} + (T_0 - T_{\text{amb}}) \cdot e^{-kt}$$
Where $T_0$ is initial meat temp (4 °C), $T_{\text{amb}}$ is external air, and $k$ represents insulation.
Bacterial Proliferation (Binary Fission Kinetics):
$$N(t) = N_0 \cdot 2^n \quad \text{where} \quad n = \sum \frac{\Delta t}{g(T)}$$
Generation interval $g(T)$ accelerates exponentially between 20 °C and 43 °C.
QMRA Dose-Response Model (FAO/WHO Standard):
$$D = N(t) \cdot m \cdot 0.01 \quad \implies \quad P_{\text{infection}} = 1 - \left(1 + \frac{D}{\beta}\right)^{-\alpha}$$
$\alpha = 0.1324, \beta = 51.45$. Translates bacterial concentration into human infection probability for a given serving portion ($m$).
